About the D3 Seminar

The Data Driven Decisions group at the University of Würzburg meets to share research in progress and exchange feedback.

The programme

Three seminars
Seminar 1, Seminar 2, and Seminar 3 during winter semester 2026/27. We reserve two hours for each session.
Onsite meeting
Two full days, 09:00–17:00, followed by a final morning, 09:00–13:00, for the whole group to meet in person. Options fall within the first two lecture weeks of summer semester 2027.
Time zone
Europe/Berlin. Dates and locations will be confirmed once scheduling is complete.

Taking part

Use your personal invitation link to enter your availability for each event on the programme page. Mark every option that works for you. For the onsite meeting, each response covers two full days and the final morning together.

We choose a time when everyone can attend. Mark each option Available or Unavailable; leave it blank if you have not decided yet. In the calendars, filled marks mean available, outlined marks mean unavailable, and empty positions await a reply. Each professor occupies one fifth of the inner ring in dark red; each student occupies one fifteenth of the outer ring in dark blue. The organizer will email the confirmed time and a calendar invitation.

The calendars

Each line around a proposed date represents one participant’s response. Select the date to see names and availability. Coloured lines within the onsite calendar connect each option’s three dates. Two dots mark full days; ½ marks the final morning, ending at 13:00. Dates shared by several options show a separate response for each option.

Dotted outlines mark proposed dates. Dashed outlines show the options with the most Available responses so far, including ties. Solid outlines mark confirmed dates. The lines joining onsite dates follow the same pattern.

Names and availability are visible to invited participants. Notes for the organizer remain private. You can update your answers until the event’s time is finalized.
