About the D3 Seminar

The Data Driven Decisions group at the University of Würzburg meets to share research in progress and exchange feedback.

Contributed talks

The programme comes together through contributions from the group. Everyone is welcome to share research updates, project talks and educational sessions. A tutorial, something learned at a summer school, or practical advice on PhD life can be just as useful as a research result.

Work in progress is welcome. You do not need a finished paper or a polished result. Share what you are working on, a question you are exploring, or a problem you are stuck on. Presenting is a chance to practise speaking, get constructive feedback and learn from one another.

Contributions are voluntary. For PhD students, a good rule of thumb is to present at least once per semester. No topic is too small to bring to the group.

Plan for a 30-minute slot, including discussion. With your personal invitation link, add your topic to the agenda and enter the minutes you need for the presentation and questions. If your topic is for a later seminar, reserve it with a provisional title. The abstract and materials can follow, and we can rearrange talks to suit the programme.

Activities together

For the onsite meeting, we also welcome ideas for hackathons, hands-on tutorials and focused group discussions. A group dataset or an open research problem can be a useful starting point. Contact the organizers if you would like to lead an activity or suggest a problem for the group to work on.

The programme

Three seminars
Seminar 1, Seminar 2, and Seminar 3 during winter semester 2026/27. We reserve two hours for each session.
Onsite meeting
Two full days, 09:00–17:00, followed by a final morning, 09:00–13:00, for the whole group to meet in person. Options fall within the first two lecture weeks of summer semester 2027.
Time zone
Europe/Berlin. Dates and locations will be confirmed once scheduling is complete.

Taking part

Use your personal invitation link to enter your availability for each event on the programme page. Mark every option that works for you. For the onsite meeting, each response covers two full days and the final morning together.

We’ll choose a time that works for everyone and email you a confirmation with a calendar invitation.

The calendars

Names and availability are visible to invited participants. Notes for the organizer remain private. You can update your answers until the event’s time is finalized.
