    Edit the event programme

    Topics, presenters, room and agenda appear to invited participants after the date is confirmed. Editing them preserves availability. Saving does not send an email.

    Participants

    Names, roles and availability are shared with invited participants. Emails and notes are only visible to organizers. Awaiting reply means unanswered, including any people still missing from the required roster.

    Private participant responses

    Email previews

    Invitations, reminders and presentation nudges use the next seminar. Final confirmations use the selected event. Sending runs locally on your computer, where you enter your university password.

    Terminal commands

    These commands prepare email previews. Add --send to deliver after entering your JMU password.

    Invite the group
    python3 scripts/mailer.py invite
    Remind only people with unfinished answers
    python3 scripts/mailer.py remind
    Nudge students to add a talk or later placeholder
    python3 scripts/mailer.py present
    After finalizing, send the selected event’s time and calendar file to everyone

    Repeated commands skip messages already sent or attempted. Test mode allows delivery only to your test address.
