    Email

    Email outlines
    1. Initial outreach · Once per person. Ask for Seminar 1 preferences by the deadline; invite optional responses for the other events.
    2. Availability invitation · Ask everyone in the selected event to choose their dates, with a deadline and personal link.
    3. Reminder · Follow up with invited people who have not finished responding.
    4. Talk proposal request · Encourage PhD participants to propose a talk, or reserve a later seminar with a provisional title.
    5. Date confirmation · Share the finalized schedule and location, with a calendar invitation attached.

    Delivery queue

    Run python3 scripts/mailer.py queued --send on your computer. It sends eligible queued messages and records delivery.

    Ready messages wait for the local sender. Attempted messages need a delivery check before any retry. Cancel stops messages that have not been claimed for sending.

    Terminal commands

    These commands prepare email previews. Add --send to deliver after entering your JMU password.

    First outreach to the group
    python3 scripts/mailer.py initial
    Invite the group to the next seminar
    python3 scripts/mailer.py invite
    Remind only people with unfinished answers
    python3 scripts/mailer.py remind
    Nudge students to add a talk or later placeholder
    python3 scripts/mailer.py present
    After finalizing, send the selected event’s time and calendar file to everyone

    Repeated commands skip messages already sent or attempted. Test mode allows delivery only to your test address.
