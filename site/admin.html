    Participants

    Private participant responses

    Send from your university account

    Review each recipient and their personalised message before sending. Reminders go only to invited people with unfinished answers. Previously sent or attempted messages are skipped.

    Used for this send only. The application does not store your password.

    Terminal commands

    These commands prepare email previews. Add --send to deliver after entering your JMU password.

    Invite the group
    python3 scripts/seminar.py send invite --all
    Remind only people with unfinished answers
    python3 scripts/seminar.py send remind --all
    After finalizing, send the time and calendar file to everyone
    python3 scripts/seminar.py send confirm --all

    Repeated commands skip messages already sent or attempted. Test mode allows delivery only to your test address.
