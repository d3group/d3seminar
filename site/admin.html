    Delivery queue

    Run python3 scripts/mailer.py queued --send on your computer. It sends eligible queued messages and records delivery.

    Ready messages wait for the local sender. Attempted messages need a delivery check before any retry. Cancel stops messages that have not been claimed for sending.

    Terminal commands

    These commands prepare email previews. Add --send to deliver after entering your JMU password.

    Invite the group
    python3 scripts/mailer.py invite
    Remind only people with unfinished answers
    python3 scripts/mailer.py remind
    Nudge students to add a talk or later placeholder
    python3 scripts/mailer.py present
